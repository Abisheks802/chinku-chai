Our Story

From a Small Garden
to Your Morning Cup

Chinku Chai was born from a simple frustration — why does the best Assam tea always end up exported abroad, while we drink leftover blends?

We travelled to the tea gardens of Assam and the lush foothills of Dooars, built direct relationships with garden owners, and set up a small packing unit that processes and ships within 48 hours of harvest.

No brokers. No auction houses. No months of storage in dusty warehouses. Just fresh, honest tea, straight from the people who grew it to the people who love it.

🌄

The Gardens We Work With

We partner with 6 small and mid-sized family estates in Assam's Brahmaputra Valley and Dooars' Jalpaiguri district — selecting only gardens with sustainable, ethical practices.

How It Works

From Leaf to Your
Morning Ritual

We've eliminated every step that compromises freshness. Here's exactly how your tea gets from garden to cup.

01
🌿

Garden Harvest

Two leaves and a bud, hand-plucked at peak season by experienced garden workers at our partner estates in Assam and Dooars.

02
🏭

48h Processing & Packing

Tea is processed (CTC, orthodox, or green) and packed in nitrogen-flushed, food-grade resealable pouches within 48 hours — never stored in bulk warehouses.

Your order is dispatched within 24 hours of placing it. We use premium courier partners to ensure safe, fast delivery across India.
