How It Works

From Leaf to Your
Morning Ritual

We've eliminated every step that compromises freshness. Here's exactly how your tea gets from garden to cup.

01
🌿

Garden Harvest

Two leaves and a bud, hand-plucked at peak season by experienced garden workers at our partner estates in Assam and Dooars.

02
🏭

48h Processing & Packing

Tea is processed (CTC, orthodox, or green) and packed in nitrogen-flushed, food-grade resealable pouches within 48 hours — never stored in bulk warehouses.

03
📦

Direct Dispatch

Your order is dispatched within 24 hours of placing it. We use premium courier partners to ensure safe, fast delivery across India.

04
☕

Your Perfect Cup

Brew, sip, and taste the difference. If you're not satisfied with the freshness in 14 days, we'll replace or refund — no questions asked.
